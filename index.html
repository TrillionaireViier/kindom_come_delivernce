The Pillars of Kingdom Come

No dragons. No magic spells. Just brutal 15th-century historical realism in the heart of the Holy Roman Empire.

⚔️ Historical European Martial Arts

Reconstructed from authentic 15th-century combat treatises by master Johannes Liechtenauer. Feints, parries, master strikes (Meisterhau), and stamina management dictate every duel.

🍺 Savior Schnapps & Mortality

No infinite auto-saves. You must drink Savior Schnapps, brewed from nettles and belladonna, sleep in a rented tavern bed, or face the consequences of your mortal decisions.

🔨 The Blacksmith's Craft

Maintain your weapon sharpness at the grindstone wheel. A dull blade bounces off Milanese plate armor, while a razor-sharp edge pierces Gambeson and splits ring mail.

🍗 Hunger, Energy & Reputation

Henry requires sustenance and sleep. Villagers react to your cleanliness, social rank, blood-splattered armor, and speech skill checks.

The Codex of Bohemia 1403

Chronicles of the Kingdom, historical figures, martial arts tenets, and survival lore.

The Captivity of King Wenceslas IV

Upon the death of Emperor Charles IV in 1378, his son Wenceslas IV inherited the throne of Bohemia. Neglecting imperial affairs in favor of leisure, he was seized and imprisoned by his scheming half-brother, King Sigismund of Hungary.

Sigismund's Cuman Army

Sigismund entered Bohemia without an army of nobles, but with fierce nomadic Cuman mercenaries from the eastern steppes. They swept into silver-rich Skalitz, putting the town and Henry's family to fire and sword.

1. Zornhau (Wrath Cut)

A diagonal descending blow from the right shoulder. Counters high thrusts and breaks into the opponent's bind with crushing leverage.

2. Krumphau (Crooked Cut)

A step across the line of attack with crossed wrists. Designed to break the Ochs (Ox) guard and strike the opponent's hands.

3. Zwerchhau (Thwart Cut)

A horizontal strike delivered with the short (false) edge under the protection of the crossguard. Nullifies overhead strikes.

4. Schielhau (Squinting Cut)

A false-edge descending strike that simultaneously parries the incoming blow and cuts the enemy's shoulder or neck.

Henry of Skalitz (Индржих)

Son of Martin the blacksmith. An ordinary blacksmith's apprentice who survives the slaughter of Skalitz and rises into the retinue of Lord Radzig Kobyla to avenge his parents.

Sir Radzig Kobyla

Hetman of Bohemia and royal burgrave of Skalitz. Loyal vassal to King Wenceslas IV, known for his wisdom, strategic patience, and secret patronage of Henry.

Sir Hans Capon (Ян Птачек)

Young and arrogant heir to the lordship of Rattay. Initially Henry's rival, their tavern brawl and subsequent hunting expedition forge an unbreakable brotherhood.

Captain Bernard

Master-at-arms in Rattay. The stern instructor who teaches Henry the fundamentals of stance, master strikes (Ripostes), and clinching.

Silver Skalitz (Stříbrná Skalice)

Prosperous mining hub nestled in the hills of silver ore. Sacked in 1403 by King Sigismund's army under Markvart von Aulitz.

Rattay upon Sasau (Rataje nad Sázavou)

Fortified settlement with twin castles: Pirkstein and the Upper Castle. Safe haven for the refugees of Skalitz under Lord Hanush.

Sasau Monastery (Sázavský klášter)

Benedictine monastery founded by Saint Procopius. Center of medical knowledge, theological disputation, and ancient Slavic liturgy.

Savior Schnapps (Спасительный Шнапс)

Ingredients: 1x Wine base, 1x Nettle, 2x Belladonna.
Brewing: Boil wine, grind nettles and boil for 2 turns. Grind belladonna and add. Distill into flask. Consuming saves game state and restores 35 Health.

Marigold Decoction

Ingredients: 1x Water base, 1x Nettle, 2x Marigold petals.
Cures battlefield lacerations, stops arterial bleeding, and slowly heals over time.
